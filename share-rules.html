Platform duration rules

Set the limits you want REAP LIFE to use before a video or audio share is created. These are editable profiles, because social-platform limits can change. REAP LIFE will warn you before a single post exceeds your chosen limit.

Important: These profiles are planning limits, not a promise that WhatsApp, Facebook or another platform will accept every media format. The platform itself remains the final authority.

How REAP LIFE will behave

  1. You choose the destination before producing the final share.
  2. REAP LIFE reads that destination's duration rule.
  3. It estimates the spoken duration from your script, or can read actual media duration when browser media metadata is available.
  4. If it fits, it shows READY — ONE POST.
  5. If it does not fit, it shows the exact amount to reduce and gives you Shorten, Edit manually, or Allow segments where enabled.
  6. Segmentation is never forced when single-post mode is selected.
